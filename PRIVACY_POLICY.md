# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 24 August 2026

This privacy policy applies to **The College View** mobile application (the "App") developed for The College View — DCU's Independent Student Newspaper.

The App is designed to provide users with access to news articles, categories, and media content published by The College View. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling any data we receive with care.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

The College View App does **not** require user registration, login, or personal account creation. As a result, we do **not** collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

The App may collect and process the following limited information:

- **Network activity data:** When you use the App, it communicates with our servers to fetch articles, images, and other content. Standard technical information such as your IP address, device type, and operating system version may be logged by our servers for security and performance purposes.
- **Usage data:** The App stores article content and images locally on your device using offline caching to improve performance. This data remains on your device and is not transmitted back to us.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

Any data collected or processed by the App is used solely for the following purposes:

- To deliver and display news articles and media content.
- To improve app performance, stability, and user experience.
- To diagnose technical issues and ensure security.

We do **not** sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

---

## 3. Data Storage and Retention

- **Local storage:** Article content, images, and preferences are cached locally on your device using secure local storage. You can clear this data by uninstalling the App or clearing the App's cache in your device settings.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

The App integrates with the following third-party services to function correctly:

- **WordPress REST API:** The App fetches published content from our WordPress-powered website. This service may collect standard server logs including IP addresses and request timestamps.
- **Image proxies:** To ensure images load correctly and comply with platform security requirements, the App may use third-party image proxy services such as `images.weserv.nl`.
- **External links:** The App contains links to external websites (including our main website, social media profiles, and messaging services). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites.

---

## 5. Permissions

The App does **not** request access to sensitive device features such as the camera, microphone, location services, photo library, contacts, or health data.

The App may request the following standard permissions:

- **Internet access:** Required to fetch articles and images.
- **Notifications:** Used to notify you when new content is available (if enabled).
- **External app launching:** The App may open external links in your web browser or other apps.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

The College View App is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information.

---

## 7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the data processed by the App and our servers. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

---

## 8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

- Request access to personal data we hold about you.
- Request correction of inaccurate data.
- Request deletion of your data.
- Withdraw consent where applicable.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details provided below.

---

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App following any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
